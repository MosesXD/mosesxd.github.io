Where the hub is going.

A portfolio-wide view of what's active, planned, paused, or still being explored. Priorities can change as research and development progress.

Status labels describe the current stage, not completion percentages. Automated checks, preview deployment, browser acceptance, merge, and production release are tracked separately.

03PLANNED

When I'm ready

  • Start visual reviews with Moses' Hub, TradeLink TT, Island Ledger, and Daymark; then work through the Caribbean prototypes.
  • Capture real screenshots and case studies only after the relevant prototype has passed its acceptance checks.
  • Validate the user needs and operating models for IslandWatch, FarmBridge Caribbean, IslandPass, SkillBridge TT, QueueLess TT, LearnLocal, ExportReady Caribbean, EventPulse Caribbean, and RentReady TT before planning production backends.

  • Soundscape: keep the fictional music-discovery demo separate from any future licensed music/API integration.
04EXPLORING

Ideas and experiments

  • Swarm Studio remains an active but lower-priority experiment; its stacked pull requests need reliable Firefox/WebKit tests before merging.
  • Caribbean Gaming Platform currently has a points-only trivia prototype. Real-money wagering, deposits, cash-out, prizes, and payments are not part of the current scope.
  • Island Life and Island State remain separate game concepts until their core loop and intended distinction are decided.
  • IslandBasket / broader local shopping-platform direction remains product planning, not a committed production build.
  • Future API, database, and new experiments stay in the backlog until current projects pass their validation gates.

No artificial deadlines.

This roadmap covers the 20-project portfolio. Finish and verify existing work before expanding scope; keep concepts separate until their purpose is clear. A working prototype is not a production service, and items move only when evidence supports the change.
