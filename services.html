PAYMENT

Deposits and deadlines.

  • A deposit of 30 to 50% starts the work. The rest is due on delivery.
  • Rush jobs can add 25 to 50%.
  • Payment details are sent privately after we agree on the scope.
  • Everything is agreed in a short written agreement before I start.

NOT YET

What I don't offer yet.

  • Online stores or anything that handles real payments
  • Logins, accounts, or security systems
  • Custom databases or complex integrations
  • Urgent fixes for live business systems
