SITE INFORMATION

Privacy policy.

Last updated: November 10, 2026

This policy explains how Moses' Hub handles information when you visit mosesxd.github.io. The site is a personal portfolio and project website.

Information and analytics

The site uses Umami analytics to understand general site usage, such as page visits and referral sources. The analytics script is configured for privacy-focused, aggregate measurement and does not use tracking cookies. Umami and its service providers may process technical information needed to provide analytics. See Umami's privacy information.

The site is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical information, including IP addresses and request logs, to deliver, protect and maintain the site. See GitHub's Privacy Statement.

Preferences stored on your device

Some site preferences, such as theme, accent, background or currency choices, may be stored locally in your browser so they can be remembered on that device. These preferences are not used by this site to identify you.

Information you send

If you contact me through an external service such as email, Discord or Instagram, I receive the information you choose to send. I use it to respond to your message or discuss a project. Those services process information under their own privacy policies.

Third-party links and embedded services

This site may link to external websites and services. When you follow those links, the third party's terms and privacy practices apply. Their sites may collect information independently of Moses' Hub.

MyAnimeList profile feature

If a MyAnimeList profile display is enabled on this site, it may request and display information from the public MyAnimeList profile associated with the username Mosess. That information is profile/list information made available by MyAnimeList, not private account data. Requests may be cached to reduce repeated API calls. MyAnimeList handles its own users' account information under its own policies. Visitors do not need to sign in to view the profile display. If this feature is not active, no MyAnimeList data is requested by the site.

Payments and accounts

Moses' Hub does not currently provide visitor accounts or accept payment details directly. Where the site links to a support or payment provider, any transaction takes place on that provider's service and is governed by its policies.

Sharing and retention

I do not sell visitors' personal information. Information sent directly to me is kept only as reasonably needed to respond, manage a project or meet applicable obligations. Technical logs and analytics data may be retained by the relevant service providers under their own retention practices.

Children

This site is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children. Please contact me if you believe a child has sent personal information through an external contact channel.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the site or its services change. The date at the top indicates when it was last reviewed.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the contact options on the Links page.
