## Privacy

**What this site collects**
- **Anonymous visit counts.** I use Umami to see how many people visit and which pages they open. It doesn't use cookies to follow you around.
- **Your choices on this device.** If you change the theme, accent color, background, or currency, the site remembers it in your own browser. That information stays on your device and isn't sent to me.
- **Hosting records.** The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may keep technical records of visits, such as IP addresses, under its own privacy statement.

**What this site doesn't do**
- It doesn't have sign-ups, accounts, or forms.
- It doesn't take payments. The Support page links out to a payment provider.
- It doesn't sell or share visitor data.

**Contacting me**
If you message me by email, Discord, or Instagram, I use what you send only to reply to you or to quote and deliver a project. Those services have their own privacy rules.

**Links to other sites**
Links to Discord, Instagram, GitHub, PayPal, and partner sites take you away from this site. Their privacy rules apply there, not mine.

## Terms of use

- The content here is for information. Nothing on this site is financial, legal, or professional advice.
- The Trading section is a preview only. It isn't an offer, and no results are promised.
- Prices on the Services page are starting ranges. A project only starts after a written agreement.
- Partner and featured spots are labelled. Paid or promoted spots are marked as ads. Their content was approved by their owners, and I'm not responsible for other sites.
- Support is a gift to help the projects. It doesn't buy anything or promise anything.
